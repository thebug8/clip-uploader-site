AutoYT

A personal tool for editing short video clips and uploading them to YouTube channels you own.

What it does

  • Loads a video clip and lets you trim it, add text and captions, and reshape it for vertical viewing.
  • Uploads the finished video to one or more of your own YouTube channels with the title, description, tags, category and visibility you choose.
  • Shows the views and likes of the videos it uploaded, and lets you edit their details.

Where it runs

AutoYT runs entirely on its user's own computer. It is not a hosted service and has no server of its own: there is nothing to sign up for on this site.

How it uses your Google account

When you link a YouTube channel, Google asks you to approve access for uploading videos to that channel, reading its basic details and statistics, and editing its videos. AutoYT uses that access only to carry out the actions you take in the app. See the privacy policy for details.
